Frequently asked questions

Can't find an answer? Email us.

Is EPMS free to use?
Yes — EPMS is completely free during open beta. When we launch the paid tier, existing teams will stay on a free plan for up to 5 members.
How many projects can I create?
Unlimited during beta. On the paid tier, the free plan includes up to 3 active projects; Pro unlocks unlimited projects and advanced analytics.
Can I import tasks from Jira or Trello?
A CSV importer is available in Settings → Import. One-click connectors for Jira and Trello are on our roadmap for Q1 2027.
Is there a mobile app?
EPMS is a responsive web app that works well on any modern phone browser. Native iOS and Android apps are planned for Q2 2027.
How does the notification system work?
You get an in-app bell notification when you are assigned a task, @mentioned in a comment, or when a task you own changes status. Daily email digests for overdue tasks can be enabled in Settings → Notifications.
What data is stored and where?
All data is stored in EU-West servers (Frankfurt, AWS). We never sell your data. Full GDPR-compliant data export is available in Settings → Danger zone → Export.
